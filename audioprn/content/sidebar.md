Free Audio Porn for and by consenting humans 18+. Links to voiced and written fictional performance art: NO Minors, NO AI, NO Limits\*

**How it works**

- **Verified creators** post links to their full and free audios and scripts. Nothing is hosted here.

Want to post your work? [Sign up as a creator](/signup/creator) with three links to your work, and an admin will verify you.

::: spoiler NO Limits - The Smallprint
Audios and scripts featuring fictional minors are NOT allowed. Do not post content that breaks laws in the United States of America; this includes incitement or encouragement to harm others or commit illegal acts, defamation, fraud, and true threats. In rare cases, audioprn may remove a post for gross insensitivity, see our full rules [here](/c/main).

The above applies to the main content page, with some very taboo tags relegated to the Dark cummunity only.

Do not post content that violates or infringes anyone else's rights, i.e. do not post copyrighted material without permission, like music and audios made by other people.

Each cummunity sets its own content and tagging rules: Content that is compliant with the main content page may not be allowed in every cummunity.

All posts must be tagged with applicable mandatory tags, including common triggers like "rape" and "incest", you can find a full list of tags that are mandatory sitewide [here](#todo). The audioprn definitions of mandatory tags are based on normally accepted conventions, i.e. incest is between blood relatives not adopted or step and rape is noncon not dynamics like consensual somno or hypno. Read our quick tagging guide [here](/guides/tagging).

All voices in an audio must consent to be included. The poster is responsible for ensuring consent. For content imported from reddit, multivoice audios are set to hidden by default and creators can unhide them at any time. Anyone wanting to opt out from the site entirely (including writers) can submit a request [here](/takedown) and admin will automate removal of every post with a mention of your reddit username and have that username permanently blocked from any future use on audioprn.net (with the exception of creator masterlists linking solely to the active reddit post). For new content, all audios must feature verified audioprn creators only. Become a creator [here](/signup/creator).
:::

::: spoiler Reporting Content

This allows us to have more permissive rules. Do not report content to admin unless it breaks [OUR rules](/c/main). :::

::: spoiler Paywall Content & Advertising
Creators are welcome to share general paywall and cash links on any post in the 'cash box' provided but snippets or teasers must be limited to their own profile posts and news feeds. Every audio and script in the cummunities must be full and free (e.g. no paywalled alternative ending). Creators breaking this rule will get ONE warning and then be permabanned.

You are welcome to mention brandnames and put a link on your content post (e.g. lovense cooperation) but keep any monetisation links to the 'cash box'. ONE warning then permaban applies to any Creator breaking our rules on how to share monetized links.
:::

::: spoiler Custom Content

::: spoiler NO Images
For moderation and liability reasons, no images are allowed on this site beyond your profile picture and Creator banner. We do not condone the use of AI images anywhere on the site but it is beyond our skills and moderation scope to enforce this effectively so we will not take action in a positive or negative way, i.e. we will neither suspend your account for AI image use nor remove comments that shame you for AI generated profile pics or banner images. We are volunteers giving you a space to share your audio and written porn in an AI-free environment, not the image police.
:::

If you use AI to write your scripts, your post text, or to voice your porn, you are not welcome here. audioprn.net is a free, volunteer-run site to preserve and celebrate human creativity. Creators breaking this rule will get TWO warnings and then be permabanned.
